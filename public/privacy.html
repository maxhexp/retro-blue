Retro Blue

Website privacy

Updated October 19, 2026

Browsing and interest forms

You can browse Retro Blue without signing in. The website stores a copy of published content and popup preferences in your browser. The interest form is hosted by Google Forms; information you submit goes to the club through that form.

Website managers

Google sign-in identifies authorized website managers. The editor checks your verified email address against its team list. It does not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or calendar.

The website stores managers’ email addresses and roles. Published revision history includes the email address of the manager who published each revision. A secure sign-in cookie expires within one hour. You can sign out to remove it sooner.

Content and uploads

Draft text is available to authorized managers. Published content is public. Uploaded images have public URLs, including images uploaded to drafts, so managers should not upload private documents or images they do not have permission to share.

Service providers

Cloudflare hosts this website and its content database. Google provides sign-in and the interest form. These services process requests under their own privacy policies: Cloudflare and Google.

Questions or removal requests

Contact the Retro Blue club organizers or a website owner about content, form responses or removal of your manager account.
