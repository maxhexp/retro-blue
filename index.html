THE LINER NOTES

About
Retro Blue

Retro Blue is LACHSA’s first-ever student-run record label, created by students, for students. We are a creative community dedicated to helping LACHSA artists turn their ideas into real opportunities while gaining hands-on experience in the music industry.

Our mission is to connect talented student musicians with the people and resources they need to grow as artists. We work with independent LACHSA artists through artist management, promotion, social media, live performances, photography, creative content, collaborations, and event production.

One of our main goals is to create more opportunities for LACHSA students to perform outside of school by producing and organizing student shows at venues throughout Los Angeles.

Kate Mori
